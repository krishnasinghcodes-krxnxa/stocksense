## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
A clinical yet warm enterprise operations platform. Designed like a high-end logistics cockpit where every metric, barcode, and ledger entry is instantly scannable, mathematically aligned, and completely devoid of generic AI slop. Built on deep slate neutrals with crisp emerald, amber, and indigo signaling.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Canvas App Background** (#090D16) — Deep logistics control-room darkness.
- **Surface Card** (#152238) — Elevated container fill with whisper borders.
- **Primary Ink** (#F8FAFC) — High-contrast text.
- **Steel Secondary** (#94A3B8) — Subdued metadata and descriptions.
- **Whisper Border** (rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)) — Structural 1px separators.
- **Accent Emerald** (#10B981) — Growth, successful receipts, healthy stock.
- **Accent Amber** (#F59E0B) — Pending deliveries, low-stock warnings.
- **Accent Rose** (#EF4444) — Out of stock, critical failures.
- **Accent Indigo** (#6366F1) — Primary CTAs and active state highlights.

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Display:** `Outfit`, `Geist`, or `Satoshi` — Track-tight, controlled fluid scale, weight-driven hierarchy.
- **Body:** System Sans-Serif / Inter alternative at weight 400 — Relaxed leading (`1.6`), max 65ch.
- **Mono:** `JetBrains Mono` or `Geist Mono` — For SKUs, reference IDs, and inventory quantities (`tabular-nums`).
- **Banned:** Generic unconsidered system stacks without hierarchy.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** Flat surface, tactile push feedback (`scale(0.98)`), accent fill with white text, no neon glows.
* **Cards:** Generously rounded (`1rem`), pure surface fill, whisper border, diffused shadow.
* **Tables:** Sticky headers, left-aligned text, right-aligned tabular numerals, clean status pills.
* **Modals:** Backdrop blur (`8px`), smooth slide-up animation, clean form grids.

## 5. Layout Principles
- **Grid-First:** CSS Grid for structural dashboard layouts.
- **No Overlapping:** Clean spatial separation across all panels.
- **Containment:** Max-width 1360px centered with generous responsive padding.

## 6. Motion & Interaction
- Spring-based micro-interactions, staggered cascade mounting for tables and lists, subtle pulse on low-stock indicators.
